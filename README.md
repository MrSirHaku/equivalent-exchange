# Equivalent Exchange

A small pixel-art alchemy game inspired by *Fullmetal Alchemist*. Gather materials, draw transmutation circles, discover recipes — and meet a few familiar faces from other anime along the way.

**Play it:** https://mrsirhaku.github.io/equivalent-exchange/

Fan game, made for fun. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators of *Fullmetal Alchemist* or any other referenced series; all characters and references belong to their respective owners.
